The Studio

Designed for
Deep Focus

Studio Forma is deliberately small. Eight Reformers, a full Tower wall, and a mat studio — nothing more. We believe better work happens in focused spaces, not crowded ones.

Maximum 8 students per Reformer class — your instructor knows your body, your history, and your goals
Balanced Body Reformers and full Cadillac Tower apparatus — professional-grade equipment maintained weekly
Private and duet sessions available by appointment for injury recovery, prenatal work, or advanced programming
No contracts, no pressure — class packs and monthly memberships with transparent pricing
